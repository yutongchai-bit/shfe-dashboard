🚨 Delivery alert & price-surge monitor — CU

Coverage = front-month top-20 OI (converted to tonnes, ×5) ÷ SHFE copper warrants. High coverage near delivery = many positions chasing little deliverable metal (squeeze conditions). Orange = backwardation.
含义: 覆盖率 = 持仓量÷可交割仓单。临近交割仍 ≥10 倍 = 「僧多粥少」，挤仓条件成立（红色警报）。 Implication: ≥10× into delivery = too many positions per tonne of registered metal — squeeze conditions (red alert).
Price surges = daily move ≥2σ vs the trailing 15-day distribution (main contract settle). Historical pattern: after UP-surges, delivery brokers cut front-month shorts ≈2,400 lots by T+3 and ≈5,200 by T+5 — they unwind into strength; sharp spikes trigger covering even at high absolute prices.
Price surges vs front-month positioning: copper price (orange, right axis) with surge days marked (▲ up-surge, ▼ down-surge); the selected scope's front-month short (red) and long (green) OI on the left axis.
含义: ▲ 之后红线下坠 = 「向强势平仓」——急涨逼出交割席位的空头回补，历史均值 T+3 约 −2,400 手、T+5 约 −5,200 手。 Implication: red line dropping after ▲ = unwind-into-strength — sharp rallies force delivery brokers to cover (historical avg ≈ −2,400 lots by T+3, −5,200 by T+5).
覆盖率路径 Coverage path: front-month OI÷warrants aligned by sessions to the delivery month (P = before, I = into). Red = current cycle with ▲/▼ marking price-surge days; grey dashed = average of previous cycles.
含义: 红线高于灰线 = 本周期比历史更"挤"；▲ 常出现在覆盖率峰值附近 —— 覆盖率飙升 → 价格急涨 → 空头平仓 → 覆盖率回落，是完整的挤仓链条。 Implication: red above grey = this cycle tighter than normal; ▲ markers cluster near coverage peaks — the full squeeze chain: coverage spikes → price surges → shorts unwind → coverage falls.
How smelters accept high prices: each bar = the average next-day change in the delivery brokers' total copper short book, grouped by where price sits in its 60-day range.
含义: 高价位组柱子为正 = 缓涨中冶炼商仍在卖出保值（接受并锁定高价）；急涨才逼出回补。若最高价位组连续转负 = 不再接受当前价格，预期更高或留货交割——重要预警。 Implication: positive bars at high price buckets = smelters keep selling forward on gradual strength (accepting/locking prices); only violent spikes force covering. 每个「当月」窗口（合约成为最近月起至其最后交易日，约上月16日→当月15日）内，前20席位在该合约上的平均买单(L)/卖单(S)与升水均值。 Each cycle window = the period a contract is the nearest month (~16th of prior month → its last trading day on the 15th); columns show top-20 avg long/short on that contract and the avg premium.
含义: 当月 S/L 比率高 + 升水低 = 空头压力周期；比率低 + 升水高 = 抢货周期。当前周期列与历史均值的偏离（%）是最直接的「本月是否异常」读数。 Implication: high S/L with low premium = short-pressure cycle; low S/L with high premium = scramble-for-metal cycle. 含义: 非前20持仓在临近交割时快速下降 = 散户/小机构照例离场；若持续偏高（红/绿实线明显高于上月虚线及灰/黄点线的近3周期均值）= 非常规资金滞留当月，交割月波动风险上升。下方差异简报表逐点列出本月 vs 近3周期均值。 Implication: residual OI normally drains into delivery (small players can't deliver); if it stays elevated vs last cycle (solid lines above dashed), unusual money is lingering in the prompt month — expiry volatility risk rises.

Front-month top-20: total shorts − total longs

Bars = top-20 short-board sum − top-20 long-board sum on the front-month contract (positive = the biggest shorts out-size the biggest longs → short-side concentration into delivery). Thin lines = the underlying top-20 long and short totals. Right axis: backwardation (orange) and premium (purple, when loaded).
含义: 与直觉相反——空头堆积（红柱）多在曲线疲软时出现（r=−0.56）；back 走扩进交割时反转为多头占优。红柱高企 + back 开始走扩 = 空头压力即将释放。 Implication: counter-intuitively, the short overhang peaks when the curve is soft (r=−0.56) and flips long-heavy as backwardation spikes; tall red bars + backwardation starting to widen = short-side pressure about to release.

Teal = net OI summed over your tracked brokers only. Right axis: backwardation (orange dashed) and your premium series (purple dashed, when loaded).
含义: 蓝线深负 + 升水走高 = 产业空头压舱、现货紧张并存；蓝线快速上穿零轴 = 空头集中撤退，往往伴随价格/升水冲高。 Implication: deeply negative blue with rising premium = heavy industry hedge base amid tightness; blue snapping up through zero = concentrated short retreat, typically alongside price/premium spikes.
